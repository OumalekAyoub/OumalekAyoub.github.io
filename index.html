02 — How I Work

Tech that answers
to the business.

Great AI isn't the most sophisticated model — it's the one that solves the problem the business actually has. That's how I work, end to end.

STEP 01
Understand the business
I start with stakeholders, not code. I run discovery sessions and workshops to learn the real problem, the constraints, and what success looks like in business terms — before a single line is written.
STEP 02
Engineer the solution
Then I bring the technical depth — AI agents, LLM pipelines, ETL systems, full-stack apps. I translate fuzzy business needs into precise, scalable technical specifications and ship them to production.
STEP 03
Drive adoption & impact
A system nobody uses has no value. I deliver demos, build tools non-technical users can run themselves, align outputs to KPIs, and make sure the work actually moves the business forward.

I'm a Senior AI Engineer based in Casablanca, Morocco — but I don't just write code. What sets my work apart is the ability to sit in a room with business stakeholders, understand what they actually need, and translate it into AI and data systems that solve the real problem.

At TELUS Digital I design and deploy production AI copilots and autonomous agents, but just as importantly I run demos, workshops, and stakeholder sessions that drive adoption across operations, product, and engineering teams. I move fluently between both worlds: writing the ETL pipeline and explaining its business impact to a director; building the model and aligning it to a KPI. My research background — a deep learning internship at ISIR, Sorbonne Université, Paris and a generative AI internship at the University of Luxembourg — gives me the technical depth, while years of client and stakeholder work give me the business fluency.

I hold a Specialized Master in Data Science from FSSM Marrakech and care most about building AI that creates measurable business value — not technology for its own sake.
